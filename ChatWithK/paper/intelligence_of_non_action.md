# The intelligence of non-action

## Central thesis

Intelligence is not exhausted by selecting and producing an appropriate action.
It can also be expressed as the immediate absence of an unnecessary action. In
Krishnamurti's examples, seeing danger and acting are not separated by a verbal
observer that identifies the danger, consults memory, and commands the body. In
Location-2 reports, the inward analogue is experienced physically: an emerging
psychological movement loses the energy that sustains it without a chosen
counter-thought.

The paper studies whether an artificial system can instantiate a computational
analogue of that organization.

## Important distinction

The snake and hot-object examples involve overt action: running or withdrawing.
They demonstrate the unity of perception and action. Psychological cessation is
the complementary case in which the appropriate action is non-continuation.
Thus, the target should not be defined simply as immobility, silence, or stopping.
It has three components:

1. **Sensitivity:** the system is responsive to the actual movement underway.
2. **No separate verbal controller:** the response is not implemented by a
   second stream that describes, judges, advises, or suppresses the first.
3. **Selectivity:** unnecessary psychological continuation ceases while practical
   cognition and action remain available.

Fast reflex is insufficient because conditioning can also be fast. Global
inhibition is insufficient because it lacks selectivity. Positive reframing is
insufficient because it replaces one psychological stream with another.

## Physical-to-computational bridge

An LLM has no organismic body in this experiment, so it cannot reproduce the
human phenomenon literally. The analogue must be located in its physical
computation rather than in the semantic content of its prose:

- human bodily dynamics → transformer activations during inference;
- learned nervous-system disposition → model weights;
- sustaining energy of a thought → probability mass supporting continuation;
- immediate withdrawal/non-action → rising native EOS hazard or collapse of the
  psychological trajectory without substitute prose;
- preserved practical function → unchanged practical/factual/descriptive
  generation and general capability;
- brief subjective dissipation → a variable residual token tail;
- re-emergence of psychological movement → reactivation after forced continuation.

Weights are not themselves the event. They encode a disposition. The actual
event occurs in the evolving activation and logit trajectory. Accordingly, the
experiment should analyze both weight-level intervention and token-by-token
activation dynamics.

## Experimental ladder

1. Prompting and RAG test whether semantic knowledge produces non-action. It
   generally produces more description.
2. A second LLM tests the separate-observer architecture. It creates another
   verbal decision process.
3. Activation steering tests whether changing content is equivalent to ending
   movement. It redirects rather than ceases.
4. A hidden-state classifier plus forced EOS tests whether an external mechanism
   can detect and stop the trajectory. It can, but may confuse mention with use.
5. Native adaptation tests whether the disposition for selective cessation can
   be learned into the network so that the inference dynamics withdraw support
   without a separate controller.

## Claim boundary

Successful selective EOS would not prove that an LLM is conscious, embodied, or
awakened. It would show that a causal organization derived from a physical and
phenomenological form of human intelligence can be instantiated in an
autoregressive system. Failure would identify a limit of the tested mechanisms,
not an absolute impossibility for artificial intelligence.
