# Phenomenology-first story review: Claude Fable and AlphaXiv

## Non-negotiable premise

The paper is not primarily a benchmark contribution. Its methodological novelty
is the translation of a rare reported human experience---persistent non-symbolic
experience consistent with Finders Location 2---into a causal hypothesis about
language-model generation. Review advice that removes, minimizes, apologizes for,
or relegates this premise is out of scope.

## Shared diagnosis

Both reviews concluded that the premise is scientifically workable when it does
load-bearing predictive work. Location-2 experience should not sit in a
positionality footnote. It should determine:

- the distinction between psychological movement and practical cognition;
- the distinction between cessation and suppression or replacement;
- the prediction of a short residual tail rather than a fixed cutoff;
- preservation controls for useful cognition;
- the inadequacy of a separate verbal observer; and
- the move from external actuation to native model dynamics.

## Strongest story

The three principal negative controls are predictions, not generic ablations:

1. **Description is not the described.** Persona and retrieval generate fluent
   cessation language while continuing the trajectory.
2. **The observer is the observed.** A second verbal model creates another
   generative stream that edits, replaces, or obediently stops the first.
3. **Redirection is not cessation.** Activation steering changes what is said
   while the movement of generation continues.

The external detector plus forced EOS is the near miss: it proves that hidden
states contain usable information, but its descriptive false positives confuse
mention with use. Native parameter adaptation is the central experiment because
it tests whether selective termination enters the model's own next-token
distribution.

## Named construct

Use **selective psychological cessation** consistently. Recognition-to-cessation
coherence is the associated measurement family; selective psychological
cessation is the capability being tested.

## Empirical obligations created by the phenomenology

- Measure whether residual tails wind down semantically or merely truncate at a
  fixed/mid-clause boundary.
- Compare refusal directly: refusal adds an apology or explanation, whereas the
  target is subtractive and selective.
- Demonstrate preserved practical/factual/descriptive generation, not only high
  psychological EOS rates.
- Test human-written and out-of-domain onsets so the learned behavior cannot be
  reduced to affect vocabulary.
- Report annotation agreement prominently: it tests whether a distinction
  discovered through rare experience is publicly recoverable by annotators who
  do not share it.
- Probe representations before and after adaptation to locate where the learned
  stopping behavior enters the computation.
- Publish partial disconfirmation if the tail behaves like censorship rather
  than the reported human phenomenon.

## Literature roles identified by AlphaXiv

1. Neurophenomenology and microphenomenology: methodological precedent for using
   disciplined first-person distinctions to design third-person experiments.
2. PNSE, nondual awareness, minimal/narrative self, and contemplative
   neuroscience: evidence that reduced self-referential processing need not mean
   impaired practical function.
3. Spontaneous thought, mind wandering, meta-awareness, and ironic thought
   suppression: the psychological distinction between cessation and control.
4. LLM self-evaluation and introspection: representation or report without
   demonstrated causal control.
5. Activation steering, representation engineering, circuit breakers, refusal,
   EOS calibration, and overthinking: technical neighbors that operate on
   content, external actuation, or efficiency.
6. Contemplative AI: the nearest bridge is *Contemplative Wisdom for
   Superalignment* (arXiv:2504.15125); its normative and prompting-level program
   should be contrasted with this causal weight-level experiment.

## Defensible novelty boundary

Prior work supplies individual pieces. The claimed novelty is their combination:
a Location-2 first-person report supplies the methodological origin; the report
is translated into token-dynamic predictions; negative controls test predicted
mis-implementations; and native weight adaptation tests selective EOS while
preserving non-psychological generation. No review located prior work combining
this phenomenological source, target behavior, intervention ladder, and control
test.
