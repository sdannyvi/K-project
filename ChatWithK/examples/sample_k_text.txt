The root of contradiction is the division between the thinker and the thought

Public Talk 5 Paris, France - 30 May 1965

I believe this is the last talk here. What is a spiritual path? Is it a path at all? Or is the very idea of "getting more spiritual" a movement of becoming — and therefore a form of contradiction? We are asking whether effort to become what we call spiritual is not still the activity of the self.
