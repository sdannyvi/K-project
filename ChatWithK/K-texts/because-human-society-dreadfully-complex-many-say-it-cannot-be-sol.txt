Because human society is dreadfully complex, many say that it cannot be solved by one central problem or issue, that you have to examine all the various aspects of it, if you can – and perhaps you will never able to solve it, that it’s impossible. You come along and say, ‘You must have an insight into the whole of it, not insight into one little part.’
