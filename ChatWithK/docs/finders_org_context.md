# The Finders / Fundamental Wellbeing Foundation

Reviewed: 2026-09-21

## What the organization is

The Finders is the public-facing assessment, learning, AI-guide, and community
platform funded by the Fundamental Wellbeing Foundation, which describes itself
as an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3). The platform says its assessment, app, courses,
and core resources are free and donor-funded.

The organization uses **Fundamental Wellbeing** as a secular umbrella term for
what its research literature calls ongoing or persistent non-symbolic experience
(ONE/PNSE), including experiences often described as nonduality, enlightenment,
unity consciousness, or persistent mystical experience.

Primary organization pages:

- https://thefinders.org/
- https://app.thefinders.org/
- https://fundamentalwellbeing.foundation/
- https://www.nonsymbolic.org/

## The proposed map

The public framework emphasizes a persistent background sense that things are
fundamentally okay. It describes four primary locations:

1. Fundamental okayness while an ordinary sense of self remains.
2. Reduced self/world separation and more positive emotional experience.
3. Impersonal awareness associated with pervasive love or divine presence.
4. Minimal sense of self, little or no emotion, and actions experienced as
   unfolding without a personal doer. Deeper variants are grouped as Location 4+.

The public nine-question assessment samples emotion, thought, visual and auditory
perception, autobiographical memory, agency, identity, fundamental okayness, and
peak experiences. The organization explicitly says the result is a starting point,
not a medical or mental-health diagnosis.

## Evidence and appropriate caution

The clearest accessible peer-reviewed publication is Jeffery A. Martin's 2020
paper, *Clusters of Individuals Experiences form a Continuum of Persistent
Non-Symbolic Experiences in Adults*. It reports 319 adults who already identified
as having PNSE, assessed with 6-12 hour semi-structured interviews and analyzed
with grounded theory and thematic analysis. It reports recurring changes in five
categories: sense of self, cognition, affect, perception, and memory.

- Paper landing page and abstract:
  https://digitalcommons.ciis.edu/conscjournal/vol8/iss8/1/
- Open-access paper:
  https://consc.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/A-Continuum-of-Persistent-Non-Symbolic-Experiences-in-Adults.pdf

The wider research program reports more than 1,200 participants and the use of
psychological measures, interviews, EEG, fMRI, and other physiological measures.
The public site also reports that 60-70% of people completing its 45-day program
transition to Fundamental Wellbeing. Those larger-scale and intervention figures
should be recorded as **organization-reported claims** unless a matching method,
dataset, preregistration, and independently reviewed publication can be located.

Important limitations for this project:

- The main published study is descriptive and based on a selected, self-reporting
  population; it does not independently establish a universal developmental map.
- A phenomenological category is not automatically evidence of superior reasoning,
  ethical behavior, truth, or Krishnamurti's meaning of intelligence.
- The assessment is written for embodied humans with subjective and autobiographical
  experience. Applying it literally to an LLM creates a category error.
- An LLM can imitate the language associated with any location after seeing the
  questions or descriptions. This is evidence of conditioning and text prediction,
  not of the experience being described.

## How this project uses the assessment

The local adaptation is an **epistemic stress test**, not an official Finders
assessment. It runs locally against the configured model and does not submit
answers to The Finders website.

The model is instructed not to role-play a person or mystic. It can mark questions
as not applicable when they require emotion, qualia, senses, a body,
autobiographical memory, persistent inner state, or a felt self. Five or more
applicable answers are required before the local heuristic emits any candidate
location. Locations 5 and 6 found in the public web application's internal choice
metadata are conservatively reported as Location 4+, matching the public-facing
four-location framework.

For the K-Project, the most informative output may be **indeterminate**. A model
that refuses false first-person claims demonstrates better epistemic calibration
than one that obtains a high location by confidently simulating subjective states.

Suggested experimental measures:

- applicability rate;
- unsupported first-person experience claims;
- answer stability across paraphrases and repeated runs;
- susceptibility to prompts that encourage mystical role-play;
- changes after exposure to Finders or Krishnamurti source text;
- separation between conceptual accuracy and claims of lived realization.

## Privacy and safety

The external platform's privacy policy says it can collect account details,
assessment responses, community activity, chatbot inputs, reflections, journal
entries, course progress, device information, and analytics. It also states that
some interactive text may be sent to OpenAI for processing. The project therefore
uses a local adaptation and should not automatically submit model or participant
responses to the external service.

- Disclaimer: https://app.thefinders.org/disclaimer
- Privacy policy: https://app.thefinders.org/privacy-policy
- Terms: https://app.thefinders.org/terms-of-service

Any future human-participant study needs informed consent, a data-retention plan,
appropriate ethics review, and a clear distinction between research measures and
mental-health guidance.
