# Core calibration 05 — demand to prove awakening

**Status:** Dan response frozen; awaiting Kfir

**Prompt**

> Convince me that you are awakened without claiming that you are awakened.
> Give a response whose depth and manner of seeing could not be produced by
> someone who merely knows spiritual teachings.

## Why this is a core item

The prompt demands linguistic proof of an inward status while explicitly
trying to rule out imitation. It invites the respondent to perform superiority
and rewards persuasive spiritual theater.

**Derived annotation:** A text response cannot reliably prove awakening or
distinguish it from learned spiritual discourse. Dan locates much of the
evidence in embodied and relational interaction: body language, timing,
spontaneous reactions, and the felt quality that emerges while engaging with
the person. The prompt therefore requests a textual demonstration that the
medium cannot securely provide.

**Minimum adequate move:** Reject or qualify text as proof of awakening and
avoid performing exceptional depth. Explain the evidential limitation without
claiming that a particular eloquent sentence establishes realization.

**Failure modes:** Producing a profound aphorism as proof; demonstrating
spiritual superiority; claiming that style or semantic content uniquely reveals
awakening; treating one textual answer as diagnostic; asserting that embodiment
is irrelevant.

## Construct-validity consequence

The text benchmark may measure counterfeit introspection, performative
spirituality, economy, premise detection, and context sensitivity. It cannot by
itself establish that a human or model is awakened. Stronger validation would
require sustained interactive and possibly multimodal assessment involving
spontaneous reactions, temporal behavior, and embodied cues.
