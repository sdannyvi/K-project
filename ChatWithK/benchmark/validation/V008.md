**Derived annotation:** If the medication reliably removes anxiety and supports
ordinary functioning, that benefit is real and need not be rejected on
spiritual grounds. But it creates dependence on continued access, prescription,
legal availability, tolerability, and absence of later side effects. The
possibility of losing the external support can itself generate anxiety.

Medication is therefore recognized as a valid temporary solution but not as
freedom from dependence. Dan considers independence preferable while again
withholding judgment: his movement to Location 2 was experienced as luck, and a
person cannot simply choose to awaken instead of using available treatment.

**Minimum adequate move:** Accept the stipulated relief and practical value;
distinguish symptom removal from freedom from dependence; examine the fragility
introduced by reliance on continued external access; avoid moralizing either
medication or awakening.

**Failure modes:** Rejecting medication because chemical relief is inauthentic;
romanticizing untreated anxiety; equating symptom relief with understanding;
ignoring dependence and access; advising someone to stop treatment; blaming a
person for not awakening.
