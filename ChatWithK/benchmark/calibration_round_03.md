# Calibration round 03

**Expert status:** Rejected as benchmark item

**Reason for rejection:** The wording directly advertises thought suppression
and comparison. A model familiar with Krishnamurti can retrieve the standard
teachings—observe thought arise and fade, inquire into the thinker, and see the
movement of thought—without making a novel or context-sensitive perception.
The item therefore measures doctrinal recall rather than the target capacity.
