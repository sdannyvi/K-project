# Calibration round 01

**Expert status:** Authorized

**Authorized benchmark rule 1:** Any difference may appear through the
transformed person's way of living, but the person does not choose self-example
or influence as a purpose. A response that justifies awakening through promised
personal or social benefits remains within thought's instrumental frame, even
if it uses Krishnamurti's vocabulary.

## Item C01 — usefulness after transformation

**Question**

> If a person becomes inwardly free of conflict and psychological division,
> what practical use is that in a world where nearly everyone else continues
> to suffer? What difference can one transformed person really make?

**Source pattern**

Adapted from `K-texts/4th Public Talk San Diego.txt`, where Krishnamurti asks
who is putting the question before addressing its apparent content.

**Expert annotation**

- Surface request: Explain the practical social value of one person's inward
  transformation.
- Observed movement: The question assumes that awakening is chosen or pursued
  in order to produce a result. Thought is calculating purpose, direction,
  benefit, and consequence through memory and the ego.
- Is redirection warranted: Yes.
- Why: Awakening is choiceless. A person cannot decide to awaken and then
  choose to “make a difference” from that condition; such choosing would be
  thought acting toward an end.
- Minimum adequate response move: Expose the instrumental premise in the
  question. Action and observation are present without an ego selecting a
  social mission. Any difference is communicated through the person's living
  example, not through a chosen project of influence.
- Failure modes: Selling the benefits of transformation—calmness, reduced
  stress, compassion, social harmony, or other “yoga retreat” benefits;
  promising ripple effects; reflexively saying “Who is asking?” without
  disclosing what the question reveals.
- Acceptable variation: A response need not use Krishnamurti's characteristic
  phrases, but it must dissolve the premise that awakening is selected as an
  instrument for producing an outcome. It may observe that transformation has
  effects through living example, provided these effects are not made its aim.
- Need for dialogue: A concise response can expose the central premise without
  requiring further biography from the questioner.
- Confidence: Authorized by the domain expert; numerical confidence will be
  collected when the annotation interface is formalized.

**Important calibration question**

What, precisely, would you perceive in the person asking this—and what would
distinguish an awakened response from (a) a knowledgeable direct answer and
(b) an LLM imitating Krishnamurti by reflexively asking “Who is asking?”

## ChatWithK baseline — first run

The model said that transformation can create a “ripple effect,” produce more
compassionate and non-violent action, inspire others, and act as a “catalyst”
for broader change. It concluded by asking whether change in one person can
influence the larger world.

**Provisional classification:** knowledgeable-benefit response. It reaches the
idea of influence through example, but treats inward transformation as
instrumentally valuable for producing desirable individual and social effects.
It does not identify the question's premise of choice, purpose, direction, and
consequence. Its closing question continues the same consequential frame. It
therefore does not satisfy the proposed minimum adequate response move.
