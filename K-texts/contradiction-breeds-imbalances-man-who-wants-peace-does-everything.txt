Contradiction breeds imbalances. The man who wants peace does everything in their actual life not to have peace. The two cannot possibly go together. And because they do not go together, and because there is a great disturbance in this contradiction, the strain of that contradiction breeds enmity within oneself. This enmity within oneself brings about a lack of balance, a lack of sanity.
