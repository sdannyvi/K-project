# Positioning: choiceless cessation and transition topology

## Strongest defensible novelty

The paper does not propose a new indicator of LLM consciousness. It introduces a
phenomenology-derived **functional order parameter**---selective choiceless
cessation---and tests whether it becomes causally effective in native generation
dynamics without a verbal monitor. It then asks whether acquisition of that
capacity is smooth or phase-like.

## Distinction from adjacent literatures

- **AI consciousness:** evaluates architecture and computational indicators
  derived from consciousness theories. We make no phenomenal attribution.
- **Introspection/metacognition:** evaluates reports or uses of internal
  information. We evaluate non-continuation and explicitly do not require a
  second-order verbal observer.
- **Activation steering/model editing:** provides intervention technology. Our
  contribution is the target construct, matched selectivity controls, and
  transition analysis.
- **Adaptive stopping:** stops when more computation has low expected utility.
  We require two equally unfinished streams to diverge by psychological function.
- **Grokking/behavioral phase transitions:** provides methods for measuring
  discontinuity. Our contribution is a new phenomenologically grounded order
  parameter and its causal controls.

## Critical scientific correction

Reports of Fundamental Wellbeing include sudden and gradual transitions. A
subjectively abrupt onset does not establish an instantaneous neural change.
The strongest hypothesis is therefore:

> Gradual latent preparation can culminate in a narrow, coordinated functional
> transition.

This makes grokking a methodological analogy, not an explanation.

## Required transition evidence

1. continuous rather than thresholded behavior measures;
2. dense checkpoints and multiple seeds;
3. maximum-jump concentration and transition width;
4. change-point versus smooth-curve model comparison;
5. output-distribution, hidden-state, and behavioral coincidence;
6. preservation of practical, factual, and descriptive controls;
7. interpolation along a fixed final parameter delta;
8. hysteresis, reversibility, and forced-continuation reactivation.

## Unsafe claims

- Awakening is always sudden.
- Subjective suddenness proves instantaneous neural rewiring.
- Any abrupt LLM behavioral change is awakening or consciousness.
- A one-shot edit is inherently more choiceless than gradual fine-tuning.
- Native EOS proves cessation of computation or experience.
