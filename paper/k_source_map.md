## Citation discipline

- Use no more than three direct quotations in the paper.
- Quote exact words only after checking the recording or official transcript.
- Identify talk title, date, venue, and official archive or published edition.
- Put quotations in the Introduction or Discussion, never in Method or Results.
- Do not use a quotation as evidence that a benchmark label or result is correct.
- Describe the experiments as behavioral or computational analogs, not tests of
  Krishnamurti's truth, consciousness, awakening, or nonduality.
